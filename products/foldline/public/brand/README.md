## Logo kit

The folded F is original artwork generated for Foldline on 19 October 2026, then traced with VTracer 0.6.15. The selected mark keeps five dominant traced color regions. Standalone edge speckles and tiny subpaths were removed, and the palette was aligned with the brand. This is actual vector geometry with no embedded raster image. Tracing simplifies the source gradients into flat faces.

Color, ink and white symbols are supplied as SVG and transparent PNG. Horizontal wordmarks include color, dark-background and one-color variants. The wordmark uses outlined Bricolage Grotesque glyphs at weight 700, shaped with HarfBuzz, so the SVG does not require a font download. The original font license is retained in WORDMARK_FONT_LICENSE.txt and the app's third-party notices.

Open index.html to inspect and download the assets. Original generated raster and trace comparisons remain in the local project evidence. Keep clear space around the mark; use one color when fold details are too small to read.
